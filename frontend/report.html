"This lease is generally compliant with tenant protection guidelines. However, several clauses should be reviewed carefully before signing."

Original Text

"Tenant agrees to pay a security deposit equal to two months' rent, which will be held in a non-interest-bearing account."

Legal Concern

Under local tenant laws, security deposits must be held in interest-bearing accounts, with interest paid annually to the tenant.

Action Suggested

Ask the landlord to specify that the deposit will be held in an interest-bearing account as required by state law.

Original Text

"Tenant may terminate lease early with a 60-day notice and payment of a fee equivalent to three months' rent."

Legal Concern

Early termination fees are capped at two months' rent under local statutes. A three-month fee is legally excessive.

Action Suggested

Negotiate to reduce the termination fee to a maximum of two months' rent to align with local tenant protections.

Original Text

"Tenant is responsible for all minor repairs under $150, including plumbing, heating, and structural issues."

Legal Concern

Landlords are legally responsible for maintaining habitability, which includes plumbing, heating, and structural components. Shifting this burden to the tenant violates state habitability codes.

Action Suggested

Request removal of plumbing and heating maintenance clauses from tenant responsibility. These essential utilities must be maintained by the landlord.
